Arize Labs is where we chase early ideas and build in the open, on a mission to
make AI work for humanity. The work falls into three threads.

## Open tooling

Open-source tools for building agents, with good practices built in.

## Open evals

Published evaluations, with the harness and data behind them, so anyone can
check the results.

## Open research

Experiments shared while in progress, with write-ups of what worked and what
didn't.
